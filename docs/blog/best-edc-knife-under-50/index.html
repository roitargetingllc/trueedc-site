Questions people ask

What blade length is best for EDC?

Somewhere between 2.5 and 3.5 inches suits most everyday tasks. Under 3 inches is the most widely accepted size.

Is D2 steel good for a pocket knife?

D2 is a common tool steel on budget and mid-range folders. It holds an edge well, but it isn't fully stainless, so wipe it dry after use.

Are knives under $50 worth it?

Yes. Brands like Kershaw, Gerber, CIVIVI and Buck make folders under $50 that people carry for years.

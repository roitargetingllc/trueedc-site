The short answerGood gifts for hikers and campers are small, light and useful when things go wrong: the Leatherman Signal multitool, a light that clips on so both hands stay free, a G-Shock that shrugs off knocks, and a notebook that works in the rain.

What outdoor people actually want

People who hike and camp count every ounce, so a gift has to replace something or solve a real problem. Big, single-use gadgets tend to stay home. Small tools that do several jobs go in the pack every time.

A multitool made for camp

The Leatherman Signal is the standout. Along with pliers, a saw and a blade, it carries a ferro rod for starting fires, a safety whistle and a hammer surface for tent stakes. For a smaller budget, the Leatherman Micra puts scissors, a small blade and tweezers on a keychain.

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for someone who loves hiking?

Something light that does more than one job, such as an outdoor multitool, a clip-on light or a weatherproof notebook.

Is the Leatherman Signal good for camping?

Yes. It is Leatherman's outdoor model, with a ferro rod, a whistle, a saw and a hammer surface among its 19 tools.

What is a good camping gift under $25?

A clip-on keychain light like the Nitecore THUMB, or a pocket notebook with a pen.

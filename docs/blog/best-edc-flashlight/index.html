Keychain light or pocket light?

A keychain light is always with you but small. A pocket light is bigger, brighter and easier to grip, and it rides on a clip instead of your keys. If you already have a keychain light and keep wishing it were brighter, you're ready for a pocket light.

How many lumens do you need?

For finding keys, reading a menu or walking a path, a few hundred lumens is plenty. High output is for scanning a yard, a trailhead or the side of a road. Most good EDC lights let you start low and step up, so a high maximum doesn't hurt, but it shouldn't be the only thing you compare.

Questions people ask

What is the best EDC flashlight?

For most people, a compact USB-C rechargeable light with a pocket clip and a range of brightness levels. The Nitecore EDC09 is our pick for its swivel head and magnetic tail.

How many lumens should an EDC flashlight have?

A few hundred lumens covers everyday tasks. More is useful outdoors or on the road, as long as the light also has low modes.

Are flat flashlights better for pocket carry?

Many people find them more comfortable, because they lie flat like a phone instead of rolling around in a pocket.

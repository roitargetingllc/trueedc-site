How to pick

Think about what he already does. A dad who fixes things wants pliers and drivers. A dad who walks the dog at night wants a light. A dad who still writes lists wants a pen that always works. Check local knife laws before gifting a blade.

Questions people ask

What is a good EDC gift for a dad who has everything?

Upgrade something he already uses, like a better pocket light or a full-size multitool, rather than adding something new.

Is a knife a good gift for a dad?

It can be, if he likes knives and they are legal where he lives. A multitool is a safer choice if you're not sure.

What EDC gift is best under $25?

A keychain flashlight or a small keychain multitool. Our stocking stuffers under $25 guide has more.

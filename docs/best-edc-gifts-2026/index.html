Questions people ask

What is an EDC gift?

An everyday carry gift is a small, useful item someone keeps on them daily, like a keychain tool, a pocket light, a pen or a simple watch.

What is a good EDC gift under $25?

The Victorinox Classic SD, an Olight iMini 2 keychain light or a Casio F-91W watch all land around $25 or less.

Is a knife an appropriate gift?

Only if the person likes knives and they are legal where they live. A multitool is the safer pick when you're unsure.

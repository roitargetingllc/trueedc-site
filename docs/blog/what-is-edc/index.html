What Is EDC? A Plain-English Guide to Everyday Carry

EDC means everyday carry: the small, useful things you keep on you every day. Here is what goes in a basic kit and how to start without overspending.

The short answerEDC stands for everyday carry: the small, useful things someone has on them every day, like keys, a wallet, a phone, a small light, a pen and a compact tool. A good EDC kit is light, legal where you live and made of things you actually use.

What counts as everyday carry

Everyday carry is less a hobby than a habit. It is whatever you pocket on the way out the door so you are not stuck when a box needs opening, the power goes out or a form needs signing.

Most kits grow from the basics everyone already carries (keys, wallet and phone) by adding one or two small tools that solve problems you run into every week.

The five pieces most kits start with

A small tool for cutting and fixing, a light that isn't your phone, a pen, something to write on and a way to keep your keys organized. You don't need all five on day one.

How to start without overspending

Start with one item that fixes a real annoyance. If you keep reaching for scissors, get a small multitool. If you keep using your phone as a flashlight, get a keychain light.

Add the next piece only when you notice you need it. A kit built that way stays light, because everything in it earns its spot. Our complete first kit under $100 is a shortcut if you'd rather start with a full set.

Questions people ask

What does EDC stand for?

EDC stands for everyday carry, meaning the items a person carries with them every day.

Is carrying a pocket knife legal?

It depends on where you are. Knife laws differ by state and city, and some places limit blade length or opening mechanism. Check your local rules, or pick a tool such as a small multitool instead.

What is the most useful EDC item?

For most people it is a small light or a compact multitool, because both solve problems that come up every week.

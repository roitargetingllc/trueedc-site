The short answerThe safest Secret Santa gifts under $25 are small things everyone uses: a keychain flashlight, an all-steel pen or a pocket notebook. None of them are blades, so they're fine for an office exchange.

Why small gear works for gift exchanges

A gift exchange has two rules nobody writes down: stay under the limit, and don't give something the person has to find a use for. Small everyday tools pass both, because they solve problems people run into every week.

We left knives off this list on purpose. Plenty of offices don't allow them, and you rarely know the person well enough to guess.

Keychain lights

Questions people ask

What is a good Secret Santa gift for a coworker?

Something small and useful that isn't personal, like a keychain flashlight, a good pen or a pocket notebook.

Is a pocket knife a good Secret Santa gift?

Usually not for an office exchange. Many workplaces don't allow knives, so a light, pen or notebook is the safer pick.

What can I get for $10?

A pair of Nite Ize S-Biner MicroLocks fits under $10. A Parker Jotter pen or a Moleskine pocket notebook costs a little more, under $20.

Questions people ask

Is a multitool or a pocket knife better for EDC?

A multitool is more versatile and more widely accepted. A pocket knife is better if most of what you do is cutting.

Which Leatherman is best for everyday carry?

For a front pocket, a smaller model like the Rev or Wingman. For people who use pliers daily, the Wave+ is the classic full-size choice.

Can I fly with a multitool?

Rules on blades and tools in carry-on bags vary by country and change over time. Check the current rules from your airport security agency before you fly, and pack it in checked bags if you are unsure.

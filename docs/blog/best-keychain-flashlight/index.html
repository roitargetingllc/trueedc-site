Questions people ask

How many lumens do I need for an EDC flashlight?

For finding keys, reading labels and walking to the car, a few hundred lumens is plenty. Higher output mainly helps outdoors and over distance.

Are rechargeable keychain lights worth it?

For most people, yes. USB-C charging means no spare batteries, and the light tops up with your phone cable.

Is a keychain light better than a phone flashlight?

It saves your phone battery, keeps your phone in your pocket and is usually brighter and easier to aim.
